    Quick rules

    Your turn
    1. Draw one card, or draw until you hold three.
    2. Play as many moves as you want.
    3. Discard down to seven, then say “Baa!”
    Make a sheep

    Match a head and butt by colour. Rainbow is wild. Paint joins different colours. Franken joins two heads or two butts.

    Actions

    Yoink lets you choose two face-down cards from an opponent's hand. Wheat steals one sheep. Wolf discards one sheep. Double-butt Franken blocks Wheat; double-head Franken blocks Wolf.

    Challenges

    Play at most one per turn, choose an opponent and guess the coin. The coin appears for everyone for five seconds, during which any player may use Re-Flip. The winner applies the challenge. Challenges left in hand are worth −3.

    BodySwap

    Select one hand half and one sheep. The resulting sheep must still be valid. The replaced half returns to your hand.

    Finish

    When the draw pile empties, everyone gets one last turn. Sheep score 1; a full rainbow scores 2.
